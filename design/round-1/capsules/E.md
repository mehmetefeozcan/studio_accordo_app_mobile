# E

**Premise (subject evidence).** The app's own strings are spoken, not labelled: "Günaydın", "Kaldığın yer", "Notalar çözümleniyor", "Geçen haftaya göre". This is the language of a lesson, addressed to one student in the second person. The page treats the interface as continuous speech set as a book page: one measured column, long leading, no headings, the instrument's facts carried inside sentences rather than in fields.

**Type.** Gambetta, roman and italic, weights 300–700. Source: Fontshare / Indian Type Foundry. License: Fontshare license, free for personal and commercial use. Fallback: Georgia, serif. Requirement was a text face with a genuine italic that reads calmly at 19px over long measure.

**Hierarchy and scale.** 40px lifted opening → 19px body → 17px indented aside → 14px colophon. Four sizes only; emphasis is carried by italic and by indent, not by scale.

**Case, weight, spacing.** Sentence case throughout; nothing uppercase except the letterspaced wordmark. Body 400 at 1.85 line-height, +0.004em. Aside hangs with a −1.7em text-indent. Tabular numerals inside prose.

**Color.** Ground `#FBF8F1` (not counted). Letterform colors: `#221F1A` primary ink (~75%), `#8A6633` secondary used only for italic voice — the aside, the quoted state, the colophon (~25%). Two active colors; the second color marks a change of speaker, not a change of importance.

**Space.** Column max-width 33em, centred, 22vh of air above the first line, 28px side padding, 140px bottom. Paragraph spacing 1.85em — one full line.

**Signature relationship.** The interface speaks in full sentences and the numbers sit inside them. Silence above the first line is as deliberate as the text.

**Invariants.** One measured column; 22vh opening air; italic + tobacco reserved for the second voice; no uppercase labels.

**Allowed variation.** Column width within 30–36em; number of paragraphs.

**Prohibited normalisation.** Breaking the column into cards, adding uppercase field labels, or using the second color for emphasis.

**Risks.** Long measure on a phone needs the em-based width re-derived for small viewports; reading-first screens suit this better than the tuner.

**Round 2 translation.** Home, progress and homework read as a page. The tuner and metronome need a dense counterpart derived from the same two voices — a risk to resolve, not a reason to abandon the premise.
