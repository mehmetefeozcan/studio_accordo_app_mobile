# D

**Premise (subject evidence).** A plucked nylon string has an immediate attack and a long decay, and the brief's first-phase features are all moments of attack followed by a tail (tune, strike, start). Each block on the page is one pluck: a word at maximum weight and minimum tracking, then the same information restated four times, each lighter, smaller and more widely tracked, until it is nearly silent. The page is right-aligned so every attack lands on the same edge and every tail trails away leftward.

**Type.** Switzer only, weights 100–900 plus italic. Source: Fontshare / Indian Type Foundry. License: Fontshare license, free for personal and commercial use. Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif. A full 100–900 range was the requirement; decay is expressed by weight, so the family had to have nine real weights.

**Hierarchy and scale.** Per block: 76px/900 → 30px/400 → 22px/300 → 16px/200 → 12px/100. Four blocks, identical envelope, different content.

**Case, weight, spacing.** Sentence case. Tracking expands as weight drops: −0.05em → +0.02em → +0.1em → +0.22em → +0.4em. Line-height opens from 0.9 to 1.6. Signature line in italic 300.

**Color.** Ground `#0B1410` (not counted). Letterform colors: `#F4F1E6` cream primary = attack and first decay step (~40%), `#6E8B75` sage secondary = the tail (~60%). Two active colors; the colour change is where the note stops being struck and starts ringing.

**Space.** 52/46/80/46px padding. 54px between plucks, 62px after the signature, 30px before the quiet coda. Right-aligned, ragged left.

**Signature relationship.** The same content loses energy down the block. Weight and tracking together make loudness visible.

**Invariants.** Right alignment; the five-step decay envelope; weight falling while tracking rises; cream for attack, sage for tail.

**Allowed variation.** Number of decay steps (three minimum); block content.

**Prohibited normalisation.** Left-aligning, flattening the envelope to two steps, or giving the tail the primary color.

**Risks.** +0.4em tracking at 12px is at the edge of comfortable reading for Turkish diacritics; needs checking at device scale.

**Round 2 translation.** Every screen is a sequence of plucks: one struck headline, then its details decaying. Transitions and sound feedback can follow the same envelope.
