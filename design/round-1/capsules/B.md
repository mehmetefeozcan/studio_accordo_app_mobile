# B

**Premise (subject evidence).** The brief requires correct grouping of aksak metres ("aksak ölçüler dahil tüm ritim türlerini doğru gruplar"). An aksak bar is unequal groups — 2+2+3 — where the first beat of each group is accented and the rest are not. The page is built as 2–2–3 blocks: gaps between items inside a group are small, gaps between groups are large, and the group-head carries the accent.

**Type.** Khand only, weights 300/400/500/600/700. Source: Fontshare / Indian Type Foundry. License: Fontshare license, free for personal and commercial use. Fallback: sans-serif. Narrow face chosen because a long word must survive at 92px on a phone-width column without shrinking.

**Hierarchy and scale.** 92px beat line → 27px sub line → 14px tail. Only three steps. Position in the group, not size, carries most of the hierarchy.

**Case, weight, spacing.** Everything uppercase. Accent = 700, unaccented = 400 or 300. Display tracking −0.012em, sub +0.07em, tail +0.3em. Line-height 0.78 on display lines so a group reads as one mass.

**Color.** Ground `#100F0D` (not counted). Letterform colors: `#EDE7DB` bone primary (~45%), `#7E7769` taupe secondary = unaccented beats (~35%), `#D8A520` amber accent = group heads (~20%). Three active colors.

**Space.** Hard left edge at 34px, no right margin — lines run to the viewport and are allowed to end where they end. 11px inside a group, 46px between groups, 58px after the wordmark, 54px before the tail.

**Signature relationship.** Vertical rhythm is unequal by rule. The reader feels 2–2–3 as spacing before reading any word.

**Invariants.** Uppercase throughout; the 2–2–3 gap ratio (small/small/large); amber reserved for group heads; the flush-left, open-right edge.

**Allowed variation.** Group contents; the metre itself (a 5/8 screen may group 2–3).

**Prohibited normalisation.** Equal gaps, centred text, a right margin, or amber used for emphasis unrelated to grouping.

**Risks.** All-uppercase Turkish needs the dotted capital İ to survive; condensed faces sometimes collide the dot with the line above at 0.78 leading.

**Round 2 translation.** Metronome screen directly. Lists elsewhere group by meaning with the same unequal gap set. Section heads are amber; secondary rows taupe.
